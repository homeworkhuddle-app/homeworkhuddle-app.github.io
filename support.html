Support

Need help, spotted a mistake, or want a method added? Email homeworkhuddle.help@outlook.com and I'll reply within two working days.

The app didn't find the sum in my photo

Use Scan homework rather than a normal photo: it flattens the page and makes the text much clearer. Handwriting and bus-stop layouts can still be hard to read, so you can always tap Type the sum instead.

My child's school sets it out differently

Schools vary slightly, especially in where they write the carried digits. In the app, go to Settings › School methods to choose your child's curriculum (British or American) and whether carried digits go above the numbers or below the answer line. Let me know what your school does differently and I'll consider adding it.

How do I share Premium with my family?

Premium works with Apple Family Sharing. Once Family Sharing is set up on your iPhone, family members can open the app and tap Settings › Restore purchases.

How do I cancel?

Open the app, go to Settings › Manage subscription, or go to the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next payment; Premium carries on until the end of the period you've paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why can't I see "Make it personal"?

Personal scripts use Apple Intelligence, which needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer on iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence switched on in Settings. Everything else in the app works on any iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
