Support

Need help, spotted a mistake, or want a method added? Email homeworkhuddle.help@outlook.com and I'll reply within two working days.

The app didn't read my homework properly

Use Scan homework rather than a normal photo: it flattens the page and makes the text much clearer. Lay the sheet flat in good light and fit the whole page in the frame. Handwriting and unusual layouts can still be hard to read, so you can always tap Type a sum for maths or Type a sentence for English instead.

It treated my English homework as maths (or the other way round)

Scan homework works out the subject for you. If it guesses wrong, tap Is this English homework? Check as English under the results, or use Type a sentence.

My child's school sets it out differently

Schools vary slightly, especially in where they write the carried digits. In the app, go to Settings › Methods and voice to choose your child's curriculum (British or American) and whether carried digits go above the numbers or below the answer line. Let me know what your school does differently and I'll consider adding it.

The spelling test voice sounds robotic

Your iPhone or iPad can download a free, natural-sounding voice. Open the Settings app, then Accessibility › Spoken Content (called Read & Speak on newer devices) › Voices › English, and download Serena (Premium) under English (UK), or Ava (Premium) under English (US). Then choose it in Homework Huddle under Settings › Methods and voice › Reading voice.

How do I share Premium with my family?

Premium works with Apple Family Sharing. Once Family Sharing is set up, family members can open the app and tap Settings › Restore purchases.

I bought Premium on another device

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app and tap Settings › Restore purchases.

How do I cancel?

Open the app, go to Settings › Manage subscription, or go to the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next payment; Premium carries on until the end of the period you've paid for. The lifetime option is a one-off purchase, so there's nothing to cancel.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why can't I see "Make it personal"?

Personal scripts use Apple Intelligence, which needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer, or an iPad with an M1 chip or newer (or iPad mini with A17 Pro), on iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence switched on in Settings. Everything else in the app works on any iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later.
