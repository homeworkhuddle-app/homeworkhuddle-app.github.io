Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Homework Huddle is made by Kash Ali, an individual developer in the United Kingdom ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the Homework Huddle app and this website.

The short version

I don't collect any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything the app does with your homework photos happens on your iPhone.

Homework photos and scans

When you scan or choose a photo, the app reads the text using Apple's on-device text recognition. The photo is not uploaded, stored by me or shared with anyone. The app does not keep a copy after reading it.

On-device AI

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, some optional features (personal read-aloud scripts, answering your questions, finding the sum in a word problem) use Apple's on-device language model. This runs on your iPhone; what you type and what the app sends to the model is not sent to me or to any other server.

Your children's names, year groups and settings

If you choose to enter your children's first names and year groups, they are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage, to personalise the app. The same goes for your settings, such as the curriculum and reading voice you choose, and the count of free sums used this week. None of this is sent to me or anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I don't receive your name, email, payment details or Apple ID. I only receive anonymous sales reports from Apple. Apple's privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

Homework Huddle is designed for parents and carers to use, not for children to use on their own. It doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with me. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies or analytics of its own. GitHub may log technical information, such as IP addresses, for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

Because I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. If you have any questions, or if you write to me by email, I'll use your email only to reply and will delete it once your question is resolved. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the date at the top.
